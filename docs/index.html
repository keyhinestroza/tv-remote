Remoto TV es un control remoto para televisores de tu red local. No recoge ningún dato. No hay cuentas, ni analítica, ni publicidad, ni componentes de terceros, y la app no envía información a sus desarrolladores ni a ningún servidor de internet.

Nothing you do here leaves your home

Last updated: 22 September 2026

TV Remote is a remote control for televisions on your local network. It collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party components, and the app sends nothing to its developers or to any server on the internet.

What is stored on your iPhone

Everything the app needs to remember stays on the device:

  • The network address and MAC address of the TV you choose
    App settings · used to connect and to switch it on over the network
  • The TV's name and model
    App settings · only to show them on the help screen
  • Your chosen language and whether you have seen the setup guide
    App settings
  • The authorisation the TV issues
    iOS Keychain · so the TV does not ask for permission on every connection

None of this leaves the iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Why the app asks for local network access

iOS asks for your permission before an app can talk to other devices on your network. TV Remote uses it for two things only: finding your television and sending it remote control commands. The app keeps no information about any other device that shows up in the search.

Children

The app collects no data, so its use by children involves no processing of personal information.
